# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include being respectful, welcoming, constructive, and open to different viewpoints. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, intimidation, and publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers through the repository's GitHub contact options. Reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially where possible.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
